Product based Quality Check

Product based Quality Check — based on QuXAT Self Check process — is designed for users/consumers of the product to check the quality of the products they use, based on a basic quality check process. The quality check review will give a QuXAT quality score to assess the quality centricity of a product.

Quality of Life – Self Assessment Platform

The Quality of Life – Self Assessment platform is a digital framework designed to help individuals, households, and communities systematically evaluate and improve their daily living standards. It provides structured self‑assessment checklists that encourage reflection, awareness, and actionable improvement across key life domains such as health, environment, safety, personal well‑being, and social engagement.
