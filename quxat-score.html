QuXAT Self‑Assessment Score (QSAS) — methodology and industry use

QSAS helps organizations quickly self‑assess core quality and safety practices. It computes a transparent score and provides guidance that maps to maturity levels, enabling immediate improvement and optional verification.

What is the QuXAT Score?

The QuXAT Score is a simple, credible indicator of compliance with essential quality and safety practices. It enables organizations to benchmark current maturity, identify gaps, and track progress over time.

How the score is computed

  • Metric selectionUp to 10 practical metrics per checklist focused on core processes.
  • Equal weightsEach “Yes” contributes an equal share; total score is out of 100.
  • Transparent thresholdsThresholds classify results into maturity bands to guide actions.

Industry use

  • Compliance readinessAlign with recognized standards; prepare for audits and certifications.
  • Operational safetySurface risk areas, strengthen safety‑critical practices, and reduce incidents.
  • Continuous improvementUse QSAS for periodic benchmarking and data‑driven improvement cycles.
